1. Dynamic Contrast Rule

                    
Top sellers maintain strong separation between shadow depths (<60 luminance)
                        and bright highlights (>190 luminance). Flops suffer from muddy, washed-out midtone
                        clustering.

                

                

                    
2. Steam Navy Saliency

                    
Steam's client background is cool navy (#1b2838). Top capsules utilize warm
                        amber, gold, and crimson focal elements (+10.9% higher warm share than flops) to naturally
                        create visual pop.

                

                

                    
3. Spotlight Vignetting

                    
71.9% of Mega-Hits use center-focused lighting with subtle perimeter darkening,
                        locking viewer gaze onto the hero subject within the 1-second Steam browsing glance.

                

                

                    
4. Sharp Silhouettes

                    
Edge density is 26.8% higher in top games (14.2% vs 11.2%). Clean character
                        outlines and distinct typographic title treatments ensure readability at 120px micro thumbnail
                        scale.

Generative Art Iteration (Midjourney & Photoshop)

                                

                                Prompt Template
                            

                            
When analyzing any game on the Home tab, Capsulu automatically
                                generates a custom prompt tailored to that capsule's exact CV deficits. Here is the
                                general structure:


                            

                                
Please optimize this attached steam capsule artwork:

1. Dynamic Contrast & Lighting:
• Push specular highlights and deepen shadows (std dev >= 63.0).

2. Color Temperature & Steam UI Pop:
• Add warm amber/golden rim-lighting to pop against Steam dark #171a21 UI.

3. Title Typography & Readability:
• Title text needs >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast against background (add drop shadow or scrim).

4. Compositional Hierarchy:
• Apply 15% radial perimeter darkening to guide eye to center hero.

5. Thumbnail Downscaling (120px Discovery Queue):
• Ensure the hero silhouette and title typography remain instantly legible when downscaled to 120px wide (as seen in Steam Discovery Queue). But do not add a thumbnail to the image.

Compliance: Adhere strictly to Steam asset rules (clean title typography only, no review quotes, no discount stickers). Do not add stuff, this needs to be the final capsule art that can be uploaded.
