1. Dynamic Contrast Rule

                    
Top sellers maintain strong separation between shadow depths (<60 luminance)
                        and bright highlights (>190 luminance). Flops suffer from muddy, washed-out midtone
                        clustering.

                

                

                    
2. Steam Navy Saliency

                    
Steam's client background is cool navy (#1b2838). Top capsules utilize warm
                        amber, gold, and crimson focal elements (+10.9% higher warm share than flops) to naturally
                        create visual pop.

                

                

                    
3. Spotlight Vignetting

                    
71.9% of Mega-Hits use center-focused lighting with subtle perimeter darkening,
                        locking viewer gaze onto the hero subject within the 1-second Steam browsing glance.

                

                

                    
4. Sharp Silhouettes

                    
Edge density is 26.8% higher in top games (14.2% vs 11.2%). Clean character
                        outlines and distinct typographic title treatments ensure readability at 120px micro thumbnail
                        scale.

                        Methodology & Neural Network Architecture
                    

                    
How Capsulu Evaluates Capsule Artwork & Estimates Sales

                    

                        Capsulu uses client-side Deep Learning Vision Neural Networks (MobileNetV3 CNN) trained on tens of thousands of Steam store capsules, analyzing raw image features to predict real-world commercial outcomes.
                    

                


                

                    
                    

                        
🧠

                        
Dual Deep Learning Vision Models

                        

                            The platform trains specialized Convolutional Neural Networks directly on raw 224×224 RGB capsule tensors:
                        

                        
    
                            
  • Indie Milestone Model: Evaluates visual cues and composition to predict probability across 5 critical review thresholds (0, 1–5, 6–10, 11–100, 100–500 reviews).
    • 
                            
  • Global Market Model: Assesses artistic rendering quality and macro commercial potential against top Steam sellers.
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
⚡

                        
Convolutional Feature Extraction

                        

                            Deep neural layers extract high-level visual representations without manual feature engineering:
                        

                        
    
                            
  • Spatial Hierarchies: Depthwise separable convolutions detect lighting accents, silhouette readability, and focal character framing.
    • 
                            
  • Pure Client-Side Inference: Models execute in WebAssembly inside the browser with ONNX Runtime Web.
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
🔓

                        
The 10 & 50 Review Thresholds

                        

                            Why review milestones matter to Steam's internal discovery algorithms:
                        

                        
    
                            
  • 10-Review Unlock: Until 10 reviews, Steam hides the review badge and limits discovery queue impressions. Crossing 10 unlocks organic traffic.
    • 
                            
  • 50-Review Traction: Proves organic interest beyond friends/family and unlocks broader recommendation carousels.
    • 
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
📈

                        
The Boxleiter / Carless Multiplier

                        

                            Steam does not publish private revenue figures, but user reviews correlate strongly with total unit sales:
                        

                        
    
                            
  • The ~30:1 Ratio: Empirical studies by Simon Carless and Jake Boxleiter show modern Steam games sell ~30 units per user review.
    • 
                            
  • Expected Sales: Calculated as Predicted Reviews × 30 with an interquartile confidence range.
    •

Generative Art Iteration (Midjourney & Photoshop)

                                

                                Prompt Template
                            

                            
When analyzing any game on the Home tab, Capsulu automatically
                                generates a custom prompt for that capsule's exact CV deficits. Here is the
                                general structure:


                            

                                
Please optimize this attached steam capsule artwork:

1. Dynamic Contrast & Lighting:
• Push specular highlights and deepen shadows (std dev >= 63.0).

2. Color Temperature & Steam UI Pop:
• Add warm amber/golden rim-lighting to pop against Steam dark #171a21 UI.

3. Title Typography & Readability:
• Title text needs >= 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast against background (add drop shadow or scrim).

4. Compositional Hierarchy:
• Apply 15% radial perimeter darkening to guide eye to center hero.

5. Thumbnail Downscaling (120px Discovery Queue):
• Ensure the hero silhouette and title typography remain instantly legible when downscaled to 120px wide (as seen in Steam Discovery Queue). But do not add a thumbnail to the image.

Compliance: Adhere strictly to Steam asset rules (clean title typography only, no review quotes, no discount stickers). Do not add stuff, this needs to be the final capsule art that can be uploaded.
